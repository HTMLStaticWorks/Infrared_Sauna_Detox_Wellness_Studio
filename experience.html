More Than a Sauna Session

A guided journey designed to help you detach from the outside world and reconnect with yourself.

01. Prepare

Leave the Noise Behind

Your journey begins the moment you enter our doors. We offer a dedicated preparation space equipped with premium amenities, soft lighting, and calming acoustics to help you detach from your day before your session even begins.

02. Warm

Embrace the Heat

Step into your private, pre-warmed environment. Whether it's the deep penetrating heat of infrared or the cellular activation of red light, this is your time to sweat, breathe, and let the warmth do its work.

03. Restore

Find Your Baseline

As the session progresses, your core temperature rises, promoting circulation and deep relaxation. This is the peak of the restorative process, allowing tension to melt away.

04. Relax

Integrate the Calm

After your session, don't rush back into the world. Our recovery lounge offers a space to rehydrate with infused waters, cool down naturally, and track your wellness progress.
