Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear to my sauna session?
We recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. During the session, most clients prefer to wear a swimsuit or the provided bamboo wrap to maximize skin exposure to the infrared light.
How often should I use the infrared sauna?
For optimal results, we suggest 2-3 sessions per week. Consistency is key to building a strong wellness routine, though even one session a week provides excellent recovery benefits.
Do I need to book an appointment in advance?
Yes, to ensure you get your preferred time and a fully prepared private suite, we highly recommend booking in advance through our online system or giving us a call.
Is red light therapy safe for everyone?
Red light therapy is completely natural, non-invasive, and safe for most people. However, if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, or take light-sensitive medications, please consult your physician first.
What is included in the recovery lounge?
Our recovery lounge includes complimentary herbal teas, infused waters, fresh towels, and a tranquil space to gradually cool down and integrate the benefits of your session before leaving.
